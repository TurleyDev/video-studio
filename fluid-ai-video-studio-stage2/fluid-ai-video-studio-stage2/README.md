# Fluid AI Video Studio — Stage 2

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. Upload an image, enter a motion prompt, choose effects, click Generate Video, and record a WebM.

Stage 2 features: prompt-keyword scene selection, three-scene storyboard, camera presets, adjustable motion/depth, animated finance charts, globe, neural network and particles, scrub/playback, 9:16/16:9/1:1 output.

**Limitations:** Effects are 2D canvas simulations (including simulated parallax/orbit), not actual scene geometry or true image-to-video AI. AI model connection and MP4 encoding are not included. Browser recording is real-time and silent.
